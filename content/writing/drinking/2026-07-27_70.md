I've relapsed. I relapsed really hard. All it took was finishing my exams. Moving back home. All the bad habits you think are dead and buried and then the rain comes, bodies wash out, and you're starring back at it. It's starring back at you. That's how it is. There's never any end to things not in a clean clinical sense. Not till you apply 9 mm of lead with appropriate force to the back of the spinal column. Not until cancer or some other illness strangles you out. Way lit by a dozen cigarettes, lighting the trial of alcohol, burn me up baby. I don't even regret it. Not even more a moment. Because that's the whole point of this. Dissolve it all into nothing. All the pain all the hurt. Leave me alone on the top of the world. I'm better this way. Give me the life of tomorrow for today. I didn't even want to live to see tomorrow anyway.

What's the point in living to see more pain anyway. Might as well burn away tomorrow in alcohol induced stupor. A good present at the low low price of getting to skip a future that would be so unbearable to deal with unvarnished. That's why I'm unrepentent. Because I've been strugling because I needed the shelter, a warm blanket counjoured by the same neurochemistry that has led humans to ferment random bullshit for generations. 

Addicts being an addict is bad of course, but then when someone tells you to set yourself straight, it just feels like someone with a better life talking down to you. Telling you what you ought to do. Telling you that your life would be better if you were them. Yeah no shit it would be but if pigs could fly. I drink because it's how I cope, how I cope with not being able to live a life like those people. And even when the pain is gone the rest of the memories bubble up to the surrface over and over again, the same thing in the present as the past. There's no getting better.
