Questions
1. How do the courts decide whether a settlor/testator intends to create a trust?
2. What is the relationship between certainty of intention and certainty of subject matter?
3. Was Dillon LJ's judgment in Hunter v Moss well-reasoned? Can it be reconciled with Re Goldcorp?
4. Bob, a novelist, died recently. Consider the validity of each of the following dispositions in his will and advise the trustees of the will, Tim and Gale, accordingly: 

(a) ‘£100,000 to my brother Louis, in absolute confidence that he will use the money to look after our mother in her time of need’;

(b) ‘I direct my trustees to hold the bulk of my residuary estate on trust for my wife, Nora; 

(c) ‘Three of my five Rembrandt sketches to be kept safe for my niece Saskia, when she reaches the age of 18’; 
(d) ‘The rights to half the novels I have written to be held on trust for Miriam’.
