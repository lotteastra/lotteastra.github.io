5. Consider the validity of the following provisions in a trust instrument: 

(a) £1,000,000 to be divided equally among the cleverest children in the London Borough of Tower Hamlets; 

This is a condition precedent, for a fixed trust. Fixed trusts require conceptual certainty and evidential certainty in order to be recognized in equity. 

IRC v. Brodway Cottages Trust -> "A trust for such members of a given class of objects as the trustees shall select is void for uncertainty, unless the whole range of objects is at least capable of ascertainment." 

Re Barlow's Wills Trust -> Testatrix left some paintings for sale but said that family or friends could purchase them at a lower rate, it is not necessary that everyone needs to be identified so long as one person can be identified then that is sufficiently certain. The object shows that they belong to the class. 

(b) £500,000 to such former employees of Queen Mary University of London, their relatives and friends as my trustees shall in their absolute discretion choose; 

This is a condition precedent for a discretionary trust. 

Re Tuck’s Settlement Trusts -> Jewish blood case, Lord Denning a chief rabbi may be acceptable to determine the question of evidence, so long as the concept was sufficiently certain. 

Eveleigh LJ -> The third party is being used as part of the conceptual certainty rule, as the definition of it. 

(c) £80,000 for my trustees to distribute among any person listed in the telephone directory under the name “Smith”, or to allocate to a hedgehog preservation society; 

These are both conditions precedent for a discretionary trust 'for my trustees to distribute,' 

Administratively unworkable 

Wilberforce suggested that a DT to benefit all residents of Greater London would fail for that reason in R v. District Audtor ex parte West Yorkshire MBC 

(d) £1,000 to be available to the trustees for the payment of sums to any person who has given up his or her place for the settlor on a bus. 

This is a condition subsequent, for a discretionary trust. 

Re Jones -> Beneficiary forfieted property if she had a social or other relationship with named person, that failed because of lack of certainty for that condition. It wasn't possible to work it out. 

This condition is conceptually certain but evidentially uncertain and unworkable as a result and the trust cannot be validly constituted.
