Re Astor's Settlement Trusts -> In a trust the object must be sufficiently identifiable so as to enforce the the trust, otherwise a trust cannot be constituted. 

"While I have reached my decision on two separatate grounds, both, I think, have their origin in a single principle, namely, that a court of equity does not as valid a trust which it cannot both enforce and control. This seems to me to be good equity and good sense." Roxburugh J
