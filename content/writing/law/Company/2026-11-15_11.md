Question 2:
Recent debates about corporate governance have demonstrated two things. First
that we should simply accept that the powers of directors to manage the company
are not to be challenged. Second, that the shareholders are no longer to be thought
of as being the owners and thus the controllers of a company, but rather they are
involved in nothing more than an investment relationship with that company and
its management. This transformation is a troubling one, which can only be to the
detriment of company law.
Critically evaluate the above statement.

!ANSWER
1. We should accept directors to manage the company should not be challenged

2. Shareholders are no longer thought of as being the owners of a company but they invest

This transformation is a troubling one

Detriment of company law
