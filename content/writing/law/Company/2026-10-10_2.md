Question 2:
“Limited liability allows companies to evade responsibility for their actions. Its cons outweigh the pros.”

Critically evaluate.

What is the purpose of company law? 
-> Facilitate enterprise
Therefore in order to do that companies need to be free to take risks in order to expand into new markets.

Limited liability, the core principle of Salomon promotes risk taking because it means that company directors will not necessarily be on the hook for company debts.

This is not always the case, in smaller companies creditors will ask for a general imdemnity, so as to secure themselves. 

However this principle is most useful in large companies, as the expenditures of a large company will crush any single individual, whereas this is less likely generally in a larger company where the liability will be limited to corporate assets. 

This facilitates enterprise by ensuring that entrepenuers will be more able to take risks and in turn facilitating enterprise.

Could be argued that allowing risks to the detriment of creditors doesn't lead to enterprise, and rather frivilous enterprises. 

However, it is still for the creditors to take action, taking strong action to curb limited liability for sham companies would needlessly undermine certainty, which is also necessary for facilitating enterprise. 

It also caps losses, so people can invest without feeling risks in cases of insolvency and also facilitates share trading
