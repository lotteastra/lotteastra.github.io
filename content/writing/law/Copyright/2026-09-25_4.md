---
title: Copyright Law Scholarly Literature Notes
---

Spence Justifications for Copyright -> He argues against most justifications for copyright in general and provides good counter arguments. However most crucially is that he highlights the things that these arguments ultimately serve. Namely that copyright law is a dispute between three different groups, authors and creators, producers and printers, libraries and educational institutions. Each group seeking to justify that copyright should move in a direction that benefits the interests of each group. 

Frye Literary Landlords -> Excellent essay which also dismantles the justifications for copyright. Arguing through analogy Frye argues that copyright in effect exists as a form of rentseeking and highlighting the monopoly dangers and the extent to which they have compounded. Also useful in that the scope and reach of copyright is incredibly broad, perhaps unjustifiably.
