Inference of direct copying

How do you find the causal connection? Look at the process of creation, and the similarities between the two different works. 

S.16(3)(b) any act done directly or indirectly. Photograph a statue and then copy the statue from that photo would be indirect copying

British Leyland v. Armstrong -> Indirect copying case. Produced a pipe based on the existing 3D pipe would infringe the drawing of the pipe. 

The court will infer copying based on the similarities. Once you do that then the burden of proof shifts for the claimant to disprove the presumption.

Deliberate copying. Find direct evidence, but hard, so find inference of deliberate copying. Look at the similarity between them. Look at the similarities between the two works.
