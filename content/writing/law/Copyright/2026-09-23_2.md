Infopaq -> Interpretation of the 2001 landmark Copyright and Information Society Directive. Infopaq sent customers summaries of Danish Newspaper Articles based on consumer preferences. DDF a professional association of Danish newspapers lodged complaints and asked Infopaq to stop as they lacked authorization. 

Infopaq disputed DDF's claim that they needed any kind of authorization. The ECJ held that Infopaq was in the wrong and their activies were not transient in nature, and therefore they required authorization from the rights holders. 

This case has had an impact on data arregation. The ECJ judgement also expanded the standard of originality went beyond those of the Berne convention but to any 'works.' Even an extract as short as 11 words was too much to be transient.
